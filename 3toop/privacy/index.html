3Toop privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

3Toop is provided by Rhythmicode, Finland. Contact: info@rhythmicode.com.

What we collect

The game does not collect or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertising or analytics, and the Android build does not request internet access.

What the game stores on your device

The game stores your settings, player profiles, progress and match records locally on your device. These files are used to restore your choices and progress. The game does not send them to Rhythmicode or other parties. Removing the app's local data, or uninstalling the app, removes these saved settings and progress.

Changes

If a future version adds online features, advertising or purchases, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@rhythmicode.com.
