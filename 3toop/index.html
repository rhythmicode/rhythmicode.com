3Toop: Street Flick Soccer

Flick one ball between the other two and toward the goal.

A football game with no players on the pitch: just three balls. On your turn, flick one ball between the other two and toward the goal.

How to play: the rules, blockers, attack items and tips for playing the CPU.

For more than half a century, 3Toop was one of the most popular games among Iranian students. They played it at school between classes, with balls and goals folded from paper and a school bench for a pitch.

“Toop” is Persian for ball: three balls, or “three toop”.
