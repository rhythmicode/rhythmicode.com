3Toop Roadmap

Where 3Toop is now and what's coming.

  • Done
  • In progress
  • Planned

Done

  1. The classic, rebuilt

    Done
    • The original 3Toop, rebuilt from scratch for today's phones
    • Two players on one screen, or the CPU at four levels
    • Stats, achievements and an awards wall

Now

  1. Blockers, attack items and new arenas

    In progress
    • Build wood, brick and rubber blockers to guard your goal
    • Fire, charged shots, Heavy and Ghost balls to break through
    • New arenas: a rainy alley, a streetball court and a parking lot
    • A graffiti look for every menu, new sounds and haptics
    • New achievements and badges

    See them in action on the How to play page.

  2. Closed testing on Android

    In progress
    • Testers play each new build on Google Play
    • Their feedback shapes the stages that follow

Next before the Android launch

  1. Learn, play, unlock

    Planned
    • A tutorial that teaches new players the game
    • Earn experience as you play and unlock new things: ice blocks, a spray-painted iron plate and a goalkeeper
    • More arenas to unlock: a rooftop, a schoolyard and a beach promenade, and the return of Pocket Park and Old Days

  2. Google Play Games

    Planned
    • Sign in with Google Play Games
    • Your achievements on your Play Games account, and leaderboards

  3. Parallel play

    Planned
    • You and the CPU take your turns at the same time
    • A mini view shows the CPU's side, and its blockers land in your game

  4. Launch on Google Play

    Planned
    • 3Toop goes live for everyone on Android

Later

  1. Play in your browser

    Planned
    • A free web version with mouse controls

  2. Steam and iPhone

    Planned
    • Steam: a free demo first, then the full game with gamepad play and Steam Deck support
    • iPhone and iPad

    Which comes first depends on how the Android and web versions go.

  3. Online play

    Planned
    • Play friends online, then ranked matches

Plans can change as we hear from players. Ideas and feedback are welcome at info@rhythmicode.com.
